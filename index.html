About the App

Kya Banaye exists to solve one small but daily source of mental load: deciding what to cook, three times a day, every single day. It's built for whoever is in charge of the kitchen — most often the women of Indian households — who carry this invisible decision-making labour without much acknowledgement.

The app suggests simple, health-conscious, 100% vegetarian meals for breakfast, lunch and dinner, built around ingredients that are ordinarily found in an Indian kitchen. It also nudges you toward small advance preparations — soaking, chopping, marinating, kneading — so that tomorrow's cooking is lighter than today's.

It does not ask you to buy anything unusual, follow a diet trend, or track calories. It is meant to remove one decision from your day, not add ten more.

What it isn't

It isn't a nutritionist, a doctor, or a dietician. It doesn't know your medical history, your allergies, your body, or your family's specific needs. Please read the note below before you rely on its suggestions.

About Us

Opal Luna Labs builds small, focused, everyday-use software — the kind that quietly saves someone a bit of time or mental effort, without asking for much in return. Kya Banaye is one such product: built around a decision millions of Indian households make silently, three times a day.

We don't collect your kitchen data on a server somewhere to sell you things — what you save in "My Kitchen" and your recipe history stay on your own device. We do intend to keep this app free to use, supported by unobtrusive advertising and an optional premium tier for people who want extra planning features; we'll always be upfront about what's free and what isn't.
